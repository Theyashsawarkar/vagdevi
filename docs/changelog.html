v0.1.02026-11-02release ↗

  • The first release: PDF, EPUB, Markdown and pictures, offline.
  • Free books from Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg and the Internet Archive, by genre and language.
  • Pages that turn like paper, at the speed you choose; tap right for next, left for back.
  • Paper, sepia, night, true black and e-ink pages.
  • PDFs open as printed; Reading mode turns them into text and pictures you can resize.
  • Contents as a full index, found by reading the pages when a PDF doesn't list its chapters.
  • A library sorted into genre shelves, with favorites, multi-select and Undo.
  • A resume mark on any paragraph, a built-in English dictionary, bookmarks.
  • A Downloads tab: progress, speed and retry. Pause, resume and cancel; downloads survive dropped connections.
  • Debug logs you can share from Settings.
